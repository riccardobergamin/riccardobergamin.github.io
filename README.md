# Riccardo Bergamin

I am a quantitative researcher working on probabilistic modelling, statistical inference, and computational methods for complex systems. My work spans two research areas: football analytics and computational genomics, with a common focus on mathematical modelling, data-driven inference, and scientific software development.

## Football Analytics

### [PRESS — Possession Risk Estimate from Spatio-temporal State](https://github.com/riccardobergamin/PRESS)

PRESS is an independent, physics-informed research and software framework for investigating defensive pressure and possession vulnerability in football.

The framework combines physics-based models of player interactions with statistical models of decision-making and possession outcomes. Using tracking and event data, it investigates possession-loss hazard, expected possession loss (xLoss), and the spatial opportunities available to the ball carrier.

**Methods:** Physics-based modelling · Probabilistic modelling · Machine learning · Spatiotemporal analysis

[Repository](https://github.com/riccardobergamin/PRESS) · [Documentation & examples](https://riccardobergamin.github.io/PRESS/)

## Computational Genomics

My research in computational genomics focuses on cancer evolution, including the reconstruction of evolutionary histories, inference of somatic event timing, and characterization of subclonal dynamics.

I develop Bayesian methods, statistical models, and scientific software for analysing genomic data. The following projects highlight research and software development that I have led within the [Caravagna Lab](https://github.com/caravagnalab).

### Selected projects

- **[TOSCA](https://github.com/caravagnalab/TOSCA)** — Bayesian inference of the timing of somatic events during cancer evolution.
- **[PEPI](https://github.com/caravagnalab/PEPI)** — Population Epigenetics and Phenotypic Inference: probabilistic modelling of phenotypic heterogeneity and evolutionary dynamics in chronic lymphocytic leukaemia.

Additional research projects, collaborations, and publications are available on my [personal website](https://riccardobergamin.github.io/).
